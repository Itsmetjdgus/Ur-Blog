Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2027-01-01

This policy explains how UrBlog operator ("we") handles personal information in Ur Blog.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider)
  • Profile: nickname (shown publicly)
  • Content you create: posts, comments, uploaded images, and reports you submit
  • Technical data: login session data stored in your browser, and server logs such as IP address kept by our service providers

Your nickname, posts, comments, and images are public. Do not use your email address as your nickname. Please do not post sensitive information about yourself or others, such as health details, addresses, or phone numbers.

2. Purposes of use

  • Creating and managing your account and signing you in
  • Publishing the content you submit
  • Handling reports, preventing spam and abuse, and securing the Service
  • Responding to your inquiries
  • Contacting you about your account, your posts, or reports concerning them, including safety concerns

Our administrators may access any content stored on the Service, only as needed for these purposes.

3. Retention and deletion

We keep your information until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your account, profile, posts, comments, and images are deleted. Copies in backups or provider logs may remain for a limited period set by the provider. We may keep limited records where required by law or to resolve disputes.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties, except where required by law.

5. Service providers and transfers abroad

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and file storage (data region: West US (Oregon), United States)
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting
  • jsDelivr: delivers a JavaScript library to your browser (this network can see your IP address)

Your information is processed and stored by Supabase in the United States to operate the Service. Items: email address, nickname, and content you submit. Purpose: hosting and operating the Service. Retention: until you delete your account. Contact urblog.contact@gmail.com with questions about this transfer.

6. Your rights

You can view and change your nickname and delete your account on your blog page, and delete your own posts and comments. To request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing, email urblog.contact@gmail.com. We respond within 14 days.

7. Cookies and local storage

We store a login session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, database access rules so that users can only change their own data, and limited administrator access.

9. Children

The Service is not for children under 14. We do not knowingly collect their information and will delete such accounts.

10. Privacy contact

Person in charge: Ur Blog operator
Email: urblog.contact@gmail.com

11. Changes

We will announce changes on the Service before they take effect.
