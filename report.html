Report content

If you see content that breaks our Terms or violates your rights, tell us.

If you have an account

Open the post or comment and use the Report button.

If you do not have an account

Email urblog.contact@gmail.com with:

  • The URL of the page
  • Which content, and how it affects your rights (for example privacy or defamation)
  • Your contact information

What happens next

We review requests promptly. We may delete the content or temporarily block access for up to 30 days, and we notify the requester and the author. Authors can ask for reinstatement by email.
